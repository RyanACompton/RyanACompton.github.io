I am Professor of Economics at the **University of Manitoba**. My research examines how institutions, economic policy, human capital, and conflict shape economic outcomes across countries and populations. My work spans development economics, conflict and defence economics, institutions, and macroeconomics. Prior to academia, I worked as an economist with the Government of Canada, including **Finance Canada**. I have also held visiting or guest appointments at the Central Bank of Brazil, Western University, National Economics University in Hanoi, and Duale Hochschule Baden-Württemberg. ## Current Research
